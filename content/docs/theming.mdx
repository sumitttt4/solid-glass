## The files

- `glaze.css`: colors, type, controls and motion. It imports `solid-glass.css`, the foundation.
- `materials.css`: Glass, Frost and Aurora.

Import both after Tailwind, and keep `solid-glass.css` beside them.

## Change the roles

Glaze keeps shadcn/ui's variable names: `--background`, `--foreground`, `--card`, `--popover`, `--primary`, `--secondary`, `--muted`, `--accent`, `--border`, `--input` and `--ring`. Change a surface and its text together, in both `:root` and `.dark`.

## Type

Manrope throughout: regular for reading, medium for controls, semibold for headings. Swap `--font-sans` and `--font-heading` to change it.

## Motion

Overlays use custom ease-out curves (`--ease-out`, `--ease-in-out`, `--ease-drawer`). Popovers grow from their trigger in 180ms and close faster, dialogs stay centered, and buttons scale to 0.97 when pressed. Reduced motion is respected everywhere.

## Check it

Look at light and dark, at desktop and phone widths, with menus open, errors showing and long labels in place.
