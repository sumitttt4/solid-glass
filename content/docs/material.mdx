## Four finishes

- **Normal** (`solid`): opaque surfaces, crisp hairlines. The default.
- **Glass**: thick, smoky glass, lit along its top edge.
- **Frost**: clear frosted glass over vivid light.
- **Aurora**: frost over light that slowly moves.

```html
<html data-material="glass">
```

Put the attribute on `<html>` for the whole app, or on any element to change only that part. Materials nest, so a Normal card inside a Glass page stays Normal.

## Give glass something to sit on

Glass reads as glass only when something is behind it. The page draws soft light of its own, and the `mat-stage` class gives any box the same backdrop. Keep long text on the thicker panes.

## Accessibility

Under the system's reduced-transparency setting, panes become nearly opaque. Under reduced motion, Aurora's light holds still.
