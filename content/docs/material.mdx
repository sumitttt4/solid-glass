## A solid foundation

Colorwork uses opaque surfaces rather than frosted glass. Background, card, panel and popover roles are distinct; tint, border and spacing establish their hierarchy.

Legacy class names such as glass-panel remain in the inherited components for compatibility. The imported Colorwork theme replaces the effects behind those names.

## Related content, related color

Use a tinted surface when it groups meaningful content. Avoid giving every item a competing saturated fill. Keep actions easy to distinguish from passive content.
