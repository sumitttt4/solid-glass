Colorwork is Sumit Sharma's evolving component library, built on the MIT-licensed Solid Glass foundation, shadcn/ui and Base UI. The name is provisional; availability has not been checked.

## Start with the component

Explore the collection on the home page. Search by component name or choose a category. Each preview is interactive, and its Code button opens a usage example you can copy into your app.

## Color has a job

Use color to establish character, group related content, show a primary action, reveal interaction state, or describe status. These roles are distinct. A green category badge does not automatically mean success.

The theme uses opaque surfaces, explicit boundaries and designed light/dark pairs. Five component tones—iris, fern, clay, sun and ocean—let related UI share a color vocabulary.

## Your code, your responsibility

The examples are local demos. They do not send email, persist projects to a server, or run an AI agent. Integrate your own data and handlers. Verify contrast and behavior in your actual product before shipping.
