## The palette

Glaze is named after ceramic glazes, and so is its palette.

- **Porcelain** `#F3F5F7`: the light background.
- **Black** `#000000`: the dark background, layered in steps (cards `#0A0A0A`, popovers `#111111`, hairlines `#1F1F1F`).
- **Ink** `#15171C`: text in light mode; soft white `#EDEDED` in dark.
- **Cobalt** `#2445E6`: the signature colour for primary actions, focus and links.
- **Celadon** `#8FC9B6`: cobalt's quiet partner, for charts and highlights.
- **Oxblood** `#B42318`: destructive actions only.

## Five tones

For categories and emphasis there are five tones: `iris` (violet), `fern` (emerald), `clay` (rose), `sun` (amber) and `ocean` (sky). Put `data-tone="fern"` on any element, or pass `tone` to a Button, ToneBadge or ColorSurface. Each tone supplies solid, soft, ink, edge and on-solid values, and a primary button inside it takes the tone's colour.

## Your own accent

Pick any accent from the palette picker on the home page, or set it in code:

```html
<html data-accent style="--accent-solid: #0f766e; --accent-on: #fff">
```

## Readable first

Keep body text at 4.5:1 or better against its real background, glass included. Colour never carries meaning alone: pair status colours with a word or an icon.
